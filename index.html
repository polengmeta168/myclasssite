Our Story

Rooted together, growing everywhere.

We started Term 1 side by side, and together we grew into something much bigger—a community of thinkers, creators, and lifelong friends. This space serves as our small corner to capture those foundational months, remembering the late-night study sessions, the shared victories, and the milestones that brought us closer. Some of us stayed close to our comfort zones, while others pushed past their limits and explored completely new ideas, but the bond formed through learning has kept us connected. Every lesson learned and every challenge overcome is a beautiful reminder that while our paths may diverge, our shared growth stays with us forever.

We started Term 1 together, and having good friends made every day better. When the lessons were difficult, we helped each other study and never felt alone. Friends are the people who laugh with you during breaks and support you when you are sad. This page is a special place to say thank you to the friends who stood by us, because learning together created a bond that we will keep forever.
